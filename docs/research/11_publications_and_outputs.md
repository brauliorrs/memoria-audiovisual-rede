# Publications and Research Outputs

This chapter defines how scientific outputs derived from **Memória Audiovisual em Rede** should be documented, cited and linked to the data and methods that produced them.

## Current status

The infrastructure is currently under operational validation. No publication should present preliminary indicators as definitive longitudinal findings until the relevant detectors, corpus decisions and analytical outputs have passed the documented validation protocol.

## Types of research output

The project may generate:

- peer-reviewed articles;
- conference papers;
- technical reports;
- methodological papers;
- public datasets;
- software releases;
- indicator releases;
- institutional reports;
- teaching and training materials;
- reproducibility packages.

## Minimum traceability requirements

Every research output based on the platform should identify, whenever applicable:

- the snapshot or snapshots used;
- the corpus version and eligibility policy;
- the collection date or observation period;
- the software release or commit;
- the indicator and methodology versions;
- known exclusions and non-assessable cases;
- whether human review was required;
- the location of the reproducibility material.

## Dataset releases

A dataset release should include:

1. a stable version identifier;
2. a manifest of included files;
3. checksums;
4. schema versions;
5. corpus composition and exclusions;
6. collection and observation dates;
7. methodological notes;
8. a licence statement;
9. citation guidance;
10. known limitations.

A workflow artifact is not, by itself, a permanent research release. Temporary artifacts support operational inspection, while durable releases must be preserved in a repository suitable for long-term citation and access.

## Indicator releases

An indicator release must preserve the exact analytical key:

```text
snapshot_id
+ indicator_id
+ indicator_version
+ methodology_version
```

Composite indexes must additionally document component definitions, weights, missing-data rules and sensitivity analysis.

## Responsible reporting

Publications must distinguish:

- detected technical evidence from verified institutional claims;
- absence of evidence from evidence of absence;
- public digital visibility from total institutional holdings;
- temporary unavailability from confirmed disappearance;
- descriptive indicators from causal explanations.

These distinctions are essential to avoid overstating what computational observation can establish.
